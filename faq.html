Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Fold Fleet: Sea Strike, naval strategy for iPhone.

Is Fold Fleet free?

Yes, it's free to download and play.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Play vs Computer works without an internet connection.

Can I play with friends?

Yes, online matches use Apple Game Center.

What devices does it support?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, with special layouts for foldable iPhone in tabletop, book and flat positions.

What's the difference between Real and Legacy mode?

Legacy is the classic grid with pegs. Real hides the grid: you aim across an open 3D ocean and watch cinematic strikes.

Does it collect my data?

No. The app collects no personal data and has no ads or tracking. Game Center is provided by Apple.

How do I control it?

Tap or drag to aim, use the on-screen arrow pad, or a keyboard (arrow keys, Return/Space to fire, 1–4 for weapons).

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes: cartoon clay ships, no blood, no chat with strangers.

How do I get support?

Visit the support page to contact the developer.
