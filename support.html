FAQ

How do I play online? Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings, then tap Play Online.

What's the difference between Real and Legacy? Real is a cinematic 3D sea battle with no board; Legacy is the classic grid with pegs.

Does it work on non-foldable iPhones? Yes — it uses the flat layout.

How do I restore a purchase? Settings → Restore purchases.
