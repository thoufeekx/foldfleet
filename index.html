What is Fold Fleet?

Fold Fleet: Sea Strike is a free naval strategy game for iPhone where you hide your fleet, hunt the enemy's ships and sink them before they sink yours — against the computer offline or against friends online with Game Center.

Who it's for

Designed for ages 9+, families, strategy fans, and foldable iPhone owners who want a fleet battle that adapts to tabletop, book, and flat play.

Choose how you fight

Play vs Computer

Take on the computer whenever you like. Single-player matches work offline, so the fleet is ready even without an internet connection.

Real mode

Fight across a boardless 3D ocean. Drag to aim, then launch missiles, airstrikes, torpedoes, or sonar and watch cinematic strikes unfold.

Legacy mode

Play the classic grid-and-pegs version of naval battleship strategy, with a familiar board and clear tactical choices.

Made for foldable iPhone

Special tabletop, book, and flat layouts make use of the screen as you change how you hold or position your foldable iPhone.

Online with Game Center

Challenge friends in online matches through Apple Game Center. Game Center handles the online player connection.

Touch, D-pad and keyboard controls

Tap or drag to aim, use the on-screen arrow pad, or play with a keyboard using the arrow keys, Return or Space, and number keys 1–4.

Quick answers

Is Fold Fleet free?

Yes, it's free to download and play.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Play vs Computer works without an internet connection.

Can I play with friends?

Yes, online matches use Apple Game Center.

What devices does it support?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, with special layouts for foldable iPhone in tabletop, book and flat positions.

What's the difference between Real and Legacy mode?

Legacy is the classic grid with pegs. Real hides the grid: you aim across an open 3D ocean and watch cinematic strikes.
