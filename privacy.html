Privacy Policy — Fold Fleet: Sea Strike

Last updated: 2026-11-05

Fold Fleet: Sea Strike (“the app”) does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

  • No app accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The app contains no third-party analytics SDKs and does not contact any server operated by the developer.
  • Game progress (levels, stars, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Online multiplayer uses Apple Game Center. Matchmaking, player names, and match messages are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive or retain this data.
  • In-app purchases, if offered, are processed by Apple's StoreKit and App Store services. The developer never sees your payment details.
  • Children: the app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
